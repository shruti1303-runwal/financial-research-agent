# NovaTech Solutions Ltd. — Annual Report FY2025

*Fictional sample company created for demonstration purposes. All figures in INR crore unless stated.*

## Letter from the CEO

FY2025 was a breakthrough year for NovaTech Solutions. Revenue grew to ₹5,120 crore from ₹4,410 crore in FY2024, driven primarily by our cloud migration services and the rapid adoption of our AI-powered analytics platform, NovaInsight. We added 142 new enterprise clients and our deal pipeline at year-end stood at ₹7,800 crore. Our CEO, Ananya Deshpande, has led the company since 2019.

We remain committed to profitable growth. Operating margin expanded by 1.4 percentage points while we continued to invest in talent and research. Looking ahead, we expect FY2026 revenue growth of 12% to 14% in constant currency.

Operating margin was 17.7% in FY2025 compared to 16.3% in FY2024. The board recommended a final dividend of ₹8 per share.

## Segment Performance

NovaTech reports three segments:

- **Cloud & Infrastructure Services:** revenue of ₹2,350 crore (FY2024: ₹1,880 crore). Growth came from large migration deals with banking and insurance clients.
- **Data & AI (NovaInsight platform):** revenue of ₹1,410 crore (FY2024: ₹1,050 crore). This is our fastest-growing segment; NovaInsight now has 310 enterprise subscribers, up from 190.
- **Application Development & Maintenance:** revenue of ₹1,360 crore (FY2024: ₹1,480 crore). This segment declined as clients shifted budgets from legacy maintenance to modernisation.

By geography, North America contributed 58% of revenue, Europe 24%, India 11% and the rest of the world 7%.

## Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

As of 31 March 2025, NovaTech held cash and equivalents of ₹1,240 crore. Total debt was ₹310 crore and shareholders' equity was ₹3,100 crore, giving a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.10. Operating cash flow was ₹820 crore and capital expenditure was ₹190 crore, resulting in free cash flow of ₹630 crore. Days sales outstanding (DSO) improved from 74 days to 68 days.

## Risks and Challenges

- **Client concentration:** our top 10 clients account for 38% of revenue.
- **Currency risk:** a 1% appreciation of the rupee against the US dollar reduces operating margin by roughly 0.3 percentage points.
- **Talent attrition:** attrition was 16.5% in FY2025 (FY2024: 19.2%); competition for AI talent remains intense.
- **Generative AI disruption:** AI coding tools may reduce demand for traditional application maintenance work, which is why we are re-skilling 6,000 employees.
- **Regulation:** new data-protection rules in India (DPDP Act) and the EU AI Act increase compliance costs.

## Strategy and Outlook

Our strategy for FY2026 focuses on three priorities: (1) scaling NovaInsight with industry-specific AI agents for banking and healthcare, (2) expanding in Europe through a new delivery centre in Kraków, Poland, and (3) improving operating margin to 18.5% through automation. We plan capital expenditure of about ₹230 crore in FY2026.

## Sustainability

NovaTech sourced 64% of its electricity from renewable sources in FY2025 (FY2024: 51%) and aims to reach net-zero operational emissions by 2032. Women make up 37% of our workforce.
